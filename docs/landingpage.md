# MASTER PROMPT — VIDYAQUEST LANDING PAGE

Act as a world-class UI/UX designer, senior frontend engineer, and product designer who has built premium websites for successful EdTech and SaaS startups.

Your task is to design and develop a **visually stunning, modern, interactive, production-quality landing page for VidyaQuest**, an AI-powered gamified education ecosystem designed to make quality education, practical learning, and opportunities accessible to students across India, especially those in rural and underserved communities.

Do not create a generic educational website. The landing page must communicate a strong product identity, inspire curiosity, build trust, and make visitors excited to explore the platform.

---

# 1. PRODUCT IDENTITY

**Product Name:** VidyaQuest

**Tagline:** Learn. Play. Explore. Achieve.

**Core Vision:**
VidyaQuest transforms traditional learning into an interactive adventure where students learn concepts, perform virtual experiments, complete challenges, develop practical skills, participate in competitions, and discover opportunities beyond their classrooms.

It is not just an LMS, online course platform, or educational game. It is a complete learning and opportunity ecosystem.

**Target Audience:**
- School students (primarily Classes 6–12).
- Teachers and educators.
- Parents and guardians.
- Schools and educational institutions.
- NGOs and CSR organizations supporting education.

**Primary Goal of the Landing Page:**
1. Explain what VidyaQuest is within the first few seconds.
2. Show how it makes learning interactive and practical.
3. Demonstrate its unique features visually.
4. Build credibility with schools, parents, and students.
5. Encourage students to get started.
6. Encourage schools and organizations to explore partnerships.

The website must feel alive, but not overwhelming.

Implement:
- Smooth scrolling.
- Sticky navigation.
- Section reveal animations.
- Subtle hover effects on cards.
- Button micro-interactions.
- Animated hero elements.
- Gentle floating illustrations.
- Scroll-based transitions where appropriate.
- Interactive feature previews.

Animations must be performant and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Avoid:
- Excessive bouncing.
- Continuous distracting movement.
- Heavy parallax.
- Too many simultaneous animations.
- Unnecessary loading screens.
